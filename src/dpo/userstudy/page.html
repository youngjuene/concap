What you will do

You will watch a series of short street clips with sound. Under each one is a sentence describing what can be heard, and a slider that changes how much that sentence ties the sound to what you can see.

For each clip: play it, then drag the slider until the sentence best matches what you are watching and hearing. Then answer two short questions. There is no right answer — we are measuring what fits, for you.
